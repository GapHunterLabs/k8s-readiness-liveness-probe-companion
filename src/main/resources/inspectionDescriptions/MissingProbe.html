Reports a container in a Kubernetes workload manifest (Deployment, Pod, StatefulSet, DaemonSet) that has no readinessProbe, no livenessProbe, or neither.

Without a readiness probe, Kubernetes keeps sending traffic to a pod that cannot serve it. Without a liveness probe, a hung process is never restarted. The check is a line-based scan of the manifest, so it works without the Kubernetes or YAML plugins.
